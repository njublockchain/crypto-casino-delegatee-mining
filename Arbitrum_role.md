**Case study on Arbitrum gambling**

ZKasino is a popular decentralized casino platform currently operating on multiple networks, mainly including Arbitrum and Polygon. It leverages the privacy and efficiency of ZK-Rollups to offer secure and anonymous gaming experiences. ZKasino eliminates the need for KYC procedures, user sign-ups, and traditional deposits or withdrawals.  Instead, players retain full control of their funds in their own wallets, placing bets directly without any intermediary involvement. With rapid transaction speeds, low fees, and seamless gameplay, ZKasino is poised to revolutionize the online gambling industry. Each game on the platform corresponds to a smart contract address, including Dice, Coin Flip, Plinko, Slots, etc. We select Coin Flip Game token(0xC4A4...E5279c) as the research object. We first identified Uniswap, SushiSwap, Paraswap, Arbswap Exchanges (with both high $Rank_{in}$ and $Rank_{out}$ values, i.e., 0x1B02...997506, 0x68B3...65FC45) and most likely gamblers (with high $Rank_{out}$ values, i.e., 0x2818...C0D2C7, 0xB28F...C9B282E) directly linked to the Coin Flip Game token address. Notably, we discovered new roles in Arbitrum networks beyond those found on TRON in Figure \ref{fig:arb}.

**Cross-chain bridges**: Cross-chain bridge is a technology framework or service that connects two or more different blockchain networks and allows assets to be transferred between them. Cross-chain Bridges(i.e., 0x80C6...99BCF8, 0xE4ED...00BCE8) help gamblers transfer ETH back and forth between the Ethereum mainnet and Arbitrum, where $Rank_{out}$ and $Rank_{in}$ exhibit approximate equality.
**Service providers**: Once assets are successfully transferred to the Arbitrum network, gamblers will then use various service providers such as liquidity pools(i.e., 0x1054...ADE261, 0x652D...AC6D97), staking platforms(i.e., 0xEA8D...4C2176, 0x1F80...E58A28), lending protocols (i.e., 0x2032...616B1F, 0xF4B1...0659E1), DAOs(i.e., 0xDA10...000DA1, 0x0998...6731C2), and NFT drop activities(i.e., 0x7BC8...0E4F3F, 0x9E6E...0D6281) to exchange their ETH for tokens that can be used for gambling.
**Game interactors**: Gamblers will exchange tokens for ZKCasino game betting tokens, with each game, such as Dice(0x66D0...777A0E), Plinko(0x067C...1B81A7), Slots(0x76CA...956521), mines(0xC21F...711E79), etc corresponding to a unique smart contract address. Gamblers will directly interact with multiple contracts separately for betting and gaming.
